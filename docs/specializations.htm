Eastern Sun Resurrected

Total Conversion Mod for Diablo II Resurrected
Mystical Shards drop from strong monsters in endgame level zones.
Transmute 10 Mystical Shards to create a Mystical Tesseract, which unlocks specializations and grants a specialization point.
More points are unlocked automatically at levels 45, 60, 75 and 90.
If your character is already over those levels, the Mystical Tesseract grants points according to your level (e.g. you get 3 points if you use it at level 63).

There are 4 specializations:
Alchemist - Specialized in potions.
Blacksmith - Specialized in weapons and armor.
Jeweler - Specialized in jewelry, gems and jewels.
Archaeologist - Specialized in drops, coupons, runes and special artefacts.

Each specialization has 15 perks, 3 perks per row. You can choose 1 perk out of 3 for each row.
You can have a maximum of 5 points.
You can respec individual perks by shift clicking a perk. This requires 5 respec points (equivalent to 20 dragon stones).
If you want to change your specialization, you need to respec all perks in the old specialization, then you will be able to pick a new specialization.
