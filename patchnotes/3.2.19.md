**Bug Fixes**

* Fixed a bug where Spirit of Nature was not invincible with heart of the oak.  
* Fixed a bug where summons would still show up as immune to elements in certain cases, despite their resistances capping at 75% (50% for phys).  
* Fixed rerolling orb not being able to output a magic warlock ring with 3-\>1 recipe.  
* Harbinger of Ruin set now has a grimoire instead of a shield.  
* Fixed a bug where bind demon would roll invalid auras.  
* Fixed a bug where gem 6packs were 2 times cheaper than intended.  
* Fixed a bug where auras on left click would not reread their current level on ticking, which made Divinity not gain skill bonuses from itself.  
* Fixed a bug where a player’s own pierce stats would count towards allies monster resistance display.  
* Fixed a crash issue related to sounds for act 4 seal bosses (leoric and lachdanan). For real this time.  
* Fixed a bug where 7 rare jewels would not transmute into 1 unique jewel outside the rerolling orb.  
* Fixed Secret Recipe 38 not working.  
* Updated introductory book with new information.  
* You can no longer downgrade scrolls 1 by 1 outside of stash to avoid ruining your scroll.

**Launcher**

* You can now configure in d2rloader settings whether you want to hide or show stat ranges on items and if you want to show maximum number of sockets or not.  
* Added a “skip all shared stash items” checkbox for rerolling orb functionality.

**Quality Affixes (Superior Affixes)**

* Completely overhauled the superior affixes system. Superior items have a much bigger pool of affixes to choose from now and they are now weighted. Rarer affixes are more powerful generally. Extended the system to more item types than before.  
* Added a new documentation page under the suffixes page called \[Quality Affixes\].

**Evil Urns**

* Added evil urns to all areas in the game that have monsters.  
* Spawn chance of urns is now 2/4/6% per room per difficulty. Room means a relatively small section of a level. You should be able to see evil urns fairly often on hell, for example.  
* Bosses inside the urns have 1 additional rare affix.  
* **Note:** 3 plugins were merged into 1, you will get a missing plugin warning. Ignore it.
