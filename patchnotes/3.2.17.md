**Bug Fixes**

* Fixed a display bug for phasing affix.  
* Fixed a bug with Verdungo’s Hearty Cord effect not stacking.  
* Fixed a bug where magic find past \~2000% was not being counted at all.  
* Fixed a bug where staves were considered polearms/spears.  
* Fixed a bug where spirit of nature was not invulnerable with heart of the oak equipped.  
* Fixed a bug where hsarus set was the one restricted to drop in cow level instead of the cow king’s set.  
* Bul kathos wedding ring and carrion wind coupons are now considered elite coupons for the purpose of rerolling.  
* Fixed culling strike on items not counting gold find, magic find and not proccing on death effects of bosses and monsters.  
* Fixed ballistas dealing 100 times more damage than intended.

**Autostocker**

* Added autostocker support for rerolling orb built in the ESRLauncher.

**Documentation**

* Removed changelogs page.  
* Added a dropdown to change modes between grouped and expanded on suffixes and prefixes page. Grouped mode is a new mode that groups affixes by item types and compacts them. (old functionality)

**Items**

* **Tinkering Points** \- Now show up on items.

**Summons**

* **All summons** are now saved on exiting the game and loaded upon entering.  
* Fixed a bug where bind demon was not saveable on a non-warlock character (e.g. augury set).

**Loot Filters**

* Fixed an issue where icons were not drawn properly on the minimap and would sometimes be drawn on the screen directly. Icons are fully supported now.  
* Fixed an issue where the light beam effects could not be colored. Now the beam can be fully colored in any color.  
* Items hidden by the filter can no longer be picked up at all.

**Gemwords**

* Reduced power growth of gemwords from 50% per gem tier to \~35%.

**Warlock**

* **Flame Wave** \- Increase direct base damage by 50% and lingering fire base damage by 100%.  
  **Seeking Flames** \- Splits an additional time now.  
* **Hellfire Bolt** \- Increased the lingering fire base damage by \~80% (more at higher levels).  
* **Miasma Bolt, Miasma Chains** \- Increased base damage over time by \~80% (more at higher levels).
