**Bug Fixes**

* Fixed a bug where lucion was still unkillable.  
* Fixed a bug where a few sounds were missing from king leoric and lachdanan in act 4 chaos sanctuary. Also attempt to fix a few related crashes.  
* Fixed Secret Recipe 50 description.  
* Fixed perfect obsidian 6pack missing the sprite.

**Loot Filters**

* Improved and enhanced the functionality of loot filters. Added new functionality.  
  * **Droplight** \- You can add droplights to rules and you can even change the color of the light now.  
  * **Sounds** \- Volume can be configured on a rule by rule basis.  
  * **Minimap Icons** \- You can now add icons for the minimap in the sprite format. They get pulled from LootFilters/icons.  
  * More features documented in the guide that ships with the new update.  
  * Known issues:  
    * Droplights not having custom colors (only come in white).  
    * Icons sometimes disappear from the map and they are drawn on the screen in certain cases.
