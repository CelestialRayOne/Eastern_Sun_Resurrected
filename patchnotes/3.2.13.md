**Bug Fixes**

* **Fixed sets accepting stats from cube recipes on their set bonuses**. Now stats are applied on the base affix pool.  
* **Fixed a bug where items with too many stats would crash the game on adding even more stats**. An obvious example of this is the kill ledger.  
* Fixed a bug where relic of knowledge was capped at 6 uses.  
* Fixed Holy Defender dealing no damage.  
* Fixed a bug where Rerolling Orb was not accepting warlock jewelry.  
* Fix attempt for king leoric crashing the game in act 4 on certain rigs.  
* Fixed some monsters spawning portals instead of using frenzy.  
* Fixed a bug where some items could roll non-existent skills, which led to blank lines on the item.  
* Fixed secret recipe 37 not working.  
* Fixed elzix selling the same arrow quiver at gamble.  
* Fixed a bug where lucion’s immunity was not breakable.  
* Reduced the defense granted by madawc’s shout.  
* Fixed a bug where Spirit of Nature was not invincible with Heart of the Oak.  
* Fixed a bug where Spirit of Nature shared the same cap as Bound Monsters.

**Documentation**

* **Added localization for all of in-game languages to the documentation as well.**  
* **Added drop finder**, a new tool that allows you to see where items can drop and what items can any monster drop.

**Cube Recipes**

* **Secret Recipe 50** \- Now requires a perfect gem instead of a Sa rune.  
* **Orb of Anointment** \- Can no longer be converted into maple leaves. Instead, it can be converted into 2 dragon stones when transmuted with a key.

**Monsters**

* Frenzy for monsters now grants significantly less damage bonus and deals less weapon damage.

**Items**

* **Ancient Scrolls** \- Reduced drop rates of scrolls by \~25%.  
* Readded gem 6packs, can downgrade them to perfect gems directly in stash.
