**Bug Fixes**

* Fixed a bug where elemental dragon stoning recipe required a forging hammer instead of a void shard.  
* Fixed a bug where characters started with a \-1% resistance penalty.  
* Added a bunch of missing in-game wiki files.  
* Fixed a tyrael might recipe being repeatable.  
* Fixed a bug where elixir of reincarnation was not craftable.  
* Hovering over physical and magic resists   
* Fixed a bug where alien artefacts could drop for anyone.  
* Fixed hireling panel being broken.  
* Fixed a bug where unique small and grand charms could not be socketed with perfect form.  
* Fixed a bug where rings and amulets socketed with sanctified jewels could not be sanctified (and rings or amulets already sanctified with a sanctified jewel instead could be sanctified again).  
* Fixed whirlwind proccing 3 times more melee attack procs than intended.  
* Fixed whirlwind crashing in rare cases.  
* Fixed a bug where jewels that gained mod through cube recipes (other than recipes that returned the same jewel back) couldn’t have their mods doubled by brilliant quality perk.

**Elixirs**

* **Diablo** \- Reduced lightning inferno damage by 50%.

**Specializations**

* **Alchemist**  
  * **Unstable Mixtures** \- Reduced chance from 15% to 10%.  
  * **Transmutation** \- Reduced chance from 50% to 25%.

**Stash**

* Added all elixirs and mystical shards to the currency tab.

**Weapon Swap Improvements**

* Now when trying to do a weapon swap while the character is in mid animation, the swap is queued and performed after the current animation finishes. Previously, the swap performed during an animation would get dropped completely, which meant you had to wait for an animation to finish and time the key press.
