**Bug Fixes**

* Fixed a bug where character saves would not load into the game main window if they went over an arbitrary 16kb limit.  
* Spirit of nature now provides spell damage as intended.  
* Added missing Worldstone Shard to the currency tab.  
* Item description for currency items (e.g. Ancient Scrolls) now show up while the item is in the stash.  
* Certain affixes no longer show on boss minions (e.g. ele enhanced, stone skin etc.) since those do not affect minions in any way.  
* Fixed a bug where hellfire torch could not be upgraded with the standard of heroes.  
* Fixed a bug where branded items could still be modified by specific recipes.  
* Fixed a bug where some Void Shard recipes were not working (for dragon stoning).  
* Fixed a bug where recipes 37 and 38 were not working.  
* Fixed a bug where champion and boss monsters would not drop loot in labyrinth of suffering.  
* Fixed a bug where ballistas and poison sentry did not work.  
* Renamed Sir Gorash (act 4 seal boss) to Lachdanan and restored his epic taunt.  
* Restored King Leoric’s (act 4 seal boss) sound set.  
* Fixed Smif’s Stone not being able to spawn warlock skills.  
* Fixed a bug where empty rows would show up in the advanced stash panel.  
* Fixed a bug where Vessel of Souls could not roll warlock skills.  
* Fixed a bug where you could equip multiple unique charms in charm inventory.  
* Fixed a bug where transmuting primordial soulstones with an ascendancy stone would not grant the correct number of souls captured, making it unable for the stone to ever ascend to tier 4\.

**Transmute**

* Now also works while the cube is closed and you can now hold the transmute hotkey to transmute constantly back to back.

**Items**

* **Raven Frost, Dwarf Star** \- Reworked.

**Mythical Uniques**

* **The Ties that Bind** \- Re-enabled. Changed them a bit, for the better.

**Barbarian**

* **Whirlwind** \- Fixed a bug where the splash part would not gain the right amount of attack rating. Reduced weapon damage from 150% to 100% and splash weapon damage from 100% to 75%. Now a level 12 skill.  
* **Frenzy** \- Reduced weapon damage from 150% to 125%. Now a level 1 skill  
* **Axe Swarm** \- Reworked into **Warpath**, now a level 30 skill. Passive, grants you a chance to summon 4 twisters on attacking while whirlwinding. Chance goes up with levels, 10% \+ 0.4% per level. Also makes frenzy attack 0.08 seconds faster at warpath levels 20, 50 and 80\. This attack speed goes beyond the fpa cap for frenzy, which makes it possible to hit a 2fpa frenzy (12.5 hits per second).

**Warlock**

* **Bind Demon** \- Fixed the demon getting malformed auras. Now it can choose from any paladin aura, except holy fire, freeze, shock.

**Monsters**

* **Stone Skin** \- Reduced duration from 10 to 5 seconds, but increased total absorb from 25% to 50% of maximum life. Total regen is halved.  
* **Teleport** \- Increased proc chance from 50% to 100% and cooldown from 2 to 4 seconds.
