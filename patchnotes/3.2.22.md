**Bug Fixes**

* Bind monsters’ life should no longer overflow. Removed the functionality that allowed monsters and summons to surpass 8.3 million hp because it was buggy and unstable. Bind monsters’ life should now cap at 8.3 million.  
* Fixed a bug with the lich king not always granting the right buff to be able to transmute the kill ledger for cannot be frozen.  
* Attempt to fix a crash related to dying.  
* Fixed an issue where Vigor was not learnable.  
* Fixed a bug where ascending stance dancer to tier 3 would sometimes not grant the stance dancing functionality. **To fix this for existing stones, please transmute the stone with itself once**. This will add the functionality to your stone.  
* Fixed a bug where Something Wicked and hellfire torch were not able to roll warlock skills.  
* Added a few missing affixes for warlock skills (grand charm skillers, warlock skills for certain items including jewels).  
* All teleport abilities that deal damage (e.g. umbral flight) have their cooldown reduced by levels now.  
* Fixed a bug where rejuvs would not be sent to the belt when shift clicking on them in the currency tab. Instead, now **ctrl** \+ left click on any beltable consumable attempts to send them in the belt first before placing them in inventory.  
* Evil urns no longer spawn the same type of monster per zone. Each urn will now roll a monster type independently of others.  
* Fixed a bug where auras on left click would sometimes deactivate.  
* Fixed a bug where auras could not be activated on controller.  
* Fixed a bug where The Soul Engine did not lower the life to 1\.  
* Fixed a bug where non-terrorized bosses in shrine of destruction were treated as being terrorized.  
* Bul Kathos’ Wedding Band now requires 4 wild cards.  
* Fixed a bug where echoing strike would fail to return if it missed an enemy while flying.

**Controller**

* Added a button for rerolling orb storing in the inventory UI.

**Filters**

* Removed the stock ESR filter (that was a copy off Sully’s filter).  
* Filters are now pulled automatically from filter creators’ repositories. If you want to have your filter featured on the launcher, pm me and I’ll add your repository. You can still add local filters, but filters curated by the launcher are seen by everyone.  
* Filters now update automatically, live, whenever a filter creator pushes changes to his repository. The update diffs against the main (default) branch.  
* Added Sully’s filter as a curated filter to the ESR Launcher.

**Chronicle**

* Unique items not in the chronicle already have additional text on their ground label that indicates that.  
* Chronicle also reads identified items now, effectively making it possible for coupon uniques to be completable.

**Gemwords**

* Fixed a bug where spell damage was significantly rarer than intended as a base random affix.  
* Indestructible, total spell and total attack damage affixes are 2 times rarer now.  
* Highest gemwords now roll between 2 and 4 affixes instead of 3 and 4 affixes.

**Treasure Goblins**

* Goblins now have a brand new custom AI which makes them significantly more intelligent at running away from the player. After a period of 30 seconds, they open a portal and disappear.  
* To compensate, their movement speed has been drastically reduced, the damage dealt by their abilities have been reduced and they no longer teleport when hurt.  
* Goblins have a 25% chance to spawn from urns compared to other monsters (spawn chance reduced by 4 times).

**Endgame Maps**

* Bosses no longer drop Primordial Soulstones after you have collected 10 of them.

**Blood Magic**

* The following skills or items drain life instead of mana while you have blood magic (if you are a blood mage): Soul Tide, Darkforce Spawn, Griffon’s Eye.  
* Attempting to cast a skill while you have blood magic and not enough life will reject the cast instead of using mana to cast it.

**Oskills**

* **Flame Rift, Ice Blink, Lightning Warp, Venom Veil, Umbral Flight, Wind Walk** \- Reduced base damage at higher levels by \~25%.

**Monster Affixes**

* **Stone Skin** \- No longer stuns players when hit. Increased cooldown from 10 to 15 seconds.

**Cube Recipes**

* Mythical uniques can now be converted to 50 ancient decipherer points in the multistocker.

**Items**

* **Death’s Fathom** \- Uncapped Vengeful Spirit’s bonus damage from cold resist.  
* **The Belt of Zeus** \- Reduced Lightning Warp oskill bonus from 10-30 to 10-15.  
* **Chillcobble** \- Increased Ice Blink oskill bonus from 5-10 to 10-15.  
* **Fireheart’s Hand** \- Added Flame Rift oskill, 10-15.  
* The 3 elite LoD warlock books now make consume last permanently and save upon exiting the game. The extra monsters bound with the help of ars’tor-baalos are also saved upon exiting the game now.  
* **Steel Carapace** \- Reduced lightning spell damage granted from 1% per 2 lightning resist to 1% per 4 lightning resist.  
* Items that grant oskill and skill bonus to the same skill (e.g. ascendancies) now sum those bonuses under an (All Classes) modifier.  
* **Girdle of Giant Strength** \- Now grants 1% bonus strength per 400 vitality instead of 1000 max life in order to avoid a feedback loop with tyrael’s might.  
* **Thawing and Antidote Potions** \- No longer grant maximum resistances.

**Paladin**

* **Fist of the Heavens** \- Fixed holy bolts not dealing damage.

**Assassin**

* Increased damage of traps significantly at lower levels and by a smaller amount at higher levels.

**Warlock**

* **Eldritch Blast** \- Fixed a bug where eldritch blast was gaining 10 times more damage per level than intended. Increased base damage by \~50% at all levels.  
* Added a bunch of warlock skills as oskills to vessel of souls.
