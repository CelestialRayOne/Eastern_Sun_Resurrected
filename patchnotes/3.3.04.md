**Bug Fixes**

* Fixed a bug where alien artefacts could not be deposited in the currency tab.  
* Fixed a bug where repairing all items could result in an error for good this time. As a side effect, repair cost displayed might be off in rare cases by a small margin (game always takes the server repair cost now).  
* Fixed a critical bug where most items that granted x% physical damage as ele damage were not working at all (e.g. Voidforge).  
* Fixed a bug where Ironbolt was getting twice the intended life on striking.  
* Fixed a bug where life pact drained twice the amount of life intended.  
* Fixed a bug where Ravens would not attack until a point in thunderclaws was learned.  
* Fixed a bug where Ravens’ default damage was not taken into consideration.  
* Fixed a bug where levitation mastery oskill (or any other passive mastery) would break auras for a very short period of time.  
* Fixed a bug where certain blacksmith nodes would bug out certain runeworded weapons.  
* Fixed a display bug where a bound monster wouldn’t list its aura in the monster display description.

**Monsters**

* **Diablo** \- Significantly reduced the damage of his lightning inferno. Reduced the damage of his fire nova by \~33%. Increased firestorm damage by \~100%.  
* **Andariel** \- Reduced her poison damage. The physical part of her poison skills no longer scales exponentially with levels.

**Items**

* Reworked a few more unique items, added a few new cool skills.

**Ascendancies**

* Removed ascendancy stones from the game.  
* Moved the entire ascendancy system in a new UI panel that can be accessed by clicking the button near the specializations (character sheet) once you unlock ascendancies.  
* The orb of creation is now a consumable that unlocks the ascendancy panel.  
* All boss kills now work off the plugin directly (based on d2rloader sdk). The kill credit should now be significantly more reliable and never fail. The kills are credited to everyone in the area of the boss. This includes kills for kill ledger bonuses and the rathma charm.  
* Entering a character that already has an ascendancy stone will instantly destroy that stone and automatically ascend that character to the intended tier (the ascendancy is transferred to the panel).  
* Changed the lich king bonus. No longer grants 1 to all skills. Instead, it now grants 10% bonus effect to the special tier 3 and 4 bonuses.  
* **Please report any bugs, this is a substantial migration.**

**Warlock**

* **Bind Monster** \- Reduced damage by \~50%. Swapping a permanent consume effect book to another book with a similar effect (e.g. ars’tor-baalos to ars’al-diabolos) will make the previous consume effect expire instantly. You will not be able to bind multiple prime evils anymore.
