**Loot Filters**

* Added loot filter support in the ESRLauncher. I took the liberty to convert Sully’s filter.  
* To use a loot filter: open the ESR Launcher, go to the Loot Filter tab, click Import filter and pick the filter's .json file. To edit one of ESR's filters, click it, then Copy to my filters, then Open folder, and edit the copy in a text editor. Changes show up in game as soon as you save.
