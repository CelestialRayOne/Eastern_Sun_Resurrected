**Barbarian**

* **Whirlwind** \- Modified the attack speed formula for whirlwind. It is now harder to reach the last attack speed breakpoints for all weapons. Extremely fast weapons (e.g. crystal swords) are now significantly slower without attack speed investment. The last IAS breakpoint is now 6 frames instead of 4 (you can now attack at most 8.33 times per second while dual wielding, down from 12.5 attacks per second, no matter how much attack speed you have; the attacks per second for a single weapon being half of that at 4.16). Now all weapons can reach the 6 fpa breakpoint with enough investment.  
  Reasoning: Whirlwind was by far the strongest and most convenient skill in the entire game. This is meant to dial back a lot of that power early game and make it slightly worse late game.
