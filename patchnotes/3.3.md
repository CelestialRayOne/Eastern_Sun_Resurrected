**Engine**

* Changed how auras grant stats. Previously, auras were not able to grant a value of 0 in a stat if it previously granted a non-zero number (it would refuse to take the 0 branch for performance reasons). Auras can now take 0 branches regardless of previous states, which means most items in the game with special effects will no longer grant a value of 1 in stats if conditions are not met. This was obvious with the kill ledger, for example. Please report any issues with auras.

**Difficulty Overhaul**

* Fixed a bug where life bonuses were lower for monsters whose levels ended in 9 (e.g. 69, 79, 89 etc.) than the previous few levels. Slightly increased life bonuses for all monsters. The life bonus should follow a much smoother curve now.  
* Damage of all monster spells and some non-spell abilities has been increased by \~20% at all levels.  
* Some spells used by monsters that were previously extremely underwhelming on higher difficulties (e.g. fallen shaman fire ball) now scale exponentially with levels, same as most spells in the game.  
* Some abilities that had spell damage now deal monsters’ weapon damage. Old elemental abilities convert the weapon damage to their element. This means some monsters will scale better with physical damage increasing effects, such as might, fanaticism auras, buffs etc.  
* Fixed a bug where the charge skill was broken for most monsters in the game. Ghastly horsemen now charge from across the screen as they did in classic ES, beware.

**Specializations**

* Added a new system called specializations. Specializations are unlocked by killing strong enemies in endgame level zones and transmuting **Mystical Shards** to get a **Mystical Tesseract** which can be used to unlock specializations and get a specialization point. More points are unlocked automatically at levels 45, 60, 75 and 90\. If your character is already over those levels, the token will grant points according to your level (e.g. you will get 3 points if you use the token at level 63).  
* There are 4 specializations:  
  * Alchemist \- Specialized on potions.  
  * Blacksmith \- Specialized on weapons and armor.  
  * Jeweler \- Specialized on jewelry, gems and jewels.  
  * Archaeologist \- Specialized in drops, coupons, runes, special artefacts.  
* Each specialization has 15 perks, 3 perks per row. You can choose 1 perk out of 3 for each row. You can have a maximum of 5 points.  
* You can respec individual perks by shift clicking a perk. This requires 5 respec points (equivalent to 20 dragon stones).  
* If you want to change your specialization, you need to respec all perks in the old specialization, then you will be able to pick a new specialization.  
* The perks are too many to list here, check them in-game.
